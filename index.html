How Flyvo is paid.

When you book through a link on Flyvo, the airline or its booking partner pays us a commission. It doesn't add anything to your fare.

Do you sell tickets?

No. Flyvo finds and compares fares, then sends you to the airline to book and pay. Your booking is with them.

Does Flyvo cost anything?

No. We earn a commission from the airline or its partner if you book, and it doesn't change the price you pay.

Are these prices live?

Not yet. Fares in this preview are samples. Live fares arrive as Flyvo connects to its flight data partner.
