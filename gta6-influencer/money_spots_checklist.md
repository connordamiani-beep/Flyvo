# GTA 6 money spots: in-game capture checklist

Goal: for each spot take **2 screenshots in your own game**:
1. the **map** with the spot pinned (open map → place waypoint on it → screenshot)
2. the **reward** on screen (cash pickup, weapon, car or watch)

PS5: press **Create** → Take Screenshot. Xbox: **Xbox button → Capture → Take screenshot**.
Name them `01_map.jpg`, `01_reward.jpg`, `02_map.jpg`… and upload to `gta6-influencer/captures/` on GitHub.

⚠️ Info below is from guides published in launch week. Amounts and details differ between sources. Verify in your game and use **your** numbers in the video.
