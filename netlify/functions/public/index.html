About us

Flight search that listens.

We love travelling, but we always found it hard to search for the trip we actually had in mind. We knew roughly what we wanted, somewhere warm, a few days away, without spending more than we had, yet every flight site wanted exact dates and a destination before it would show us anything. So we built the search we wished we'd had.

Flyvo is for people who know their budget before they know their destination. Tell it what you can spend and when you're free, in your own words, and it finds trips that fit, from a weekend away to a week of winter sun.

We compare prices across airlines, point out the trips worth your money, and send you straight to the airline or booking partner to book. Flyvo is free to use. We may earn a small commission when you book, at no extra cost to you.
